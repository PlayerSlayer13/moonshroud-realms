# Aric Vhantor

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Aric directly*  
*Usually found: Bastion of Valor*

---

Dragonborn Knight-Commander of the Bastion of Valor and leader of the local chapter of the
Red Ramparts, the order of Sartrian paladins renowned for holding the line against
overwhelming odds. Earned his position through years of service rather than political
influence.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Aric's command of the Bastion and the Red Ramparts is well known among
Bridgeport's soldiers, guards, and mercenaries, but the party hasn't dealt with him directly.

## Reputation

Disciplined, composed, and difficult to rattle even in a crisis. Direct almost to a fault,
and known for judging people by their conduct rather than their station. Rarely quotes
scripture when speaking of courage — he prefers to recount the deeds of ordinary people who
stood firm when retreat would have been easier.
