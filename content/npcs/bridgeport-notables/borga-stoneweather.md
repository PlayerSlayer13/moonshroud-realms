# Borga Stoneweather

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Borga directly*  
*Usually found: Thatchers', Roofers' & Masons' Circle guildhall, Mill Road*

---

Dwarven master builder and Guildmaster of the Thatchers', Roofers' & Masons' Circle.
Coordinates many of the projects shaping Bridgeport's physical landscape, from foundations
and warehouses to bridges and public buildings, and is known as one of the city's foremost
authorities on construction and civic works.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Borga's guild oversees a good deal of the city's construction, but the
party hasn't had any reason to deal with her directly.

## Reputation

Practical, direct, and proud of good craftsmanship wherever she finds it. Known to appear
unannounced at construction sites to the quiet dismay of less exacting foremen, and fond of
reminding apprentices that a properly built structure should outlive its builder.
