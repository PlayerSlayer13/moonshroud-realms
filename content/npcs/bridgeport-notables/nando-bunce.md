# Nando Bunce

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Nando directly*  
*Usually found: Sleepy Stag Tavern, near Moonwillow Gate*

---

Halfling proprietor of the Sleepy Stag Tavern, one of northeastern Bridgeport's more
comfortable and refined inns. Personable and discreet, with a good memory for guests and
their business.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. The party hasn't stayed at or visited the Sleepy Stag.

## Reputation

Calm and welcoming even with a full house, known for keeping a quieter atmosphere than the
busier dockside taverns and for not asking more of his guests than good manners require.
