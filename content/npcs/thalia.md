# Thalia Craypot

*First encountered: The Strangled Goat, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*  
*Usually found: The Strangled Goat, Saltstone Square*

---

One of the waitstaff at the **Strangled Goat** in Saltstone Square. 
Efficient, observant, and possessed of the particular calm that comes 
from working a busy tavern long enough that very little surprises you 
anymore.

## What the Party Knows

Thalia got the party their first round at the Goat without being asked 
twice. She pointed those looking for work toward **Velra**, the 
Adventurer's Guild representative working from one of the nooks near 
the main entrance — the kind of quiet, useful information that suggests 
she knows exactly what kind of people walk through that door and what 
they usually need.

She did not appear troubled by Vail dancing on the table.

## Impressions

Professional. Familiar with the tavern's regulars and its rhythms. The 
sort of person who notices things and files them away without making a 
production of it.
