# Annallee Shellcana

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Annallee directly*  
*Usually found: Shellcana Botanical Garden, near Moonwillow Creek*

---

Aelfaen herbalist, botanist, and collector of rare flora who owns and tends the Shellcana
Botanical Garden near Moonwillow Creek. Graceful and intensely focused, with a near-obsessive
fascination for unusual plant life gathered from distant forests, marshes, ruins, and foreign
ports over years of collecting.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Annallee's garden and her reputation as one of the region's foremost
herbalists are known well beyond Bridgeport, but the party hasn't crossed paths with her.

## Reputation

Regarded as exceptionally curious and brilliant, with a memory for plant specimens that
borders on encyclopedic. Keeps an extensive stock of rare herbs, spell components, and
alchemical ingredients, and pays well for unusual samples brought back from dangerous
places — a useful contact for anyone who comes back from the wilds with something strange
growing out of their pack.
