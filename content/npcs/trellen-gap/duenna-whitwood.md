# Duenna Whitwood

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Whitwood Timber Camp, along Cord Creek*

---

A sharp-eyed halfling who owns and manages several carefully maintained woodlots throughout
the forest around Trellen Gap, Duenna coordinates crews and assigns daily cuts from her camp
along Cord Creek. Her commitment to sustainable practice isn't sentimental — she understands
plainly that overcutting today means no livelihood tomorrow, and enforces strict, deliberately
small and separated boundaries on where her crews work.

Blunt and relentlessly active, Duenna is rarely still, walking her claims most days to inspect
progress and mark future cuts — sometimes covering twenty miles in a single day despite her
stature. She knows her crews by name, has little patience for carelessness, and keeps a keen
ear for anything odd in the deeper woods; not superstitious exactly, but she doesn't ignore a
pattern she can't explain.
