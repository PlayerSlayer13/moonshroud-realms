# Session 06 — A Dead Courier, a Box of Moonstone, and a Very Polite Offer to Escort Them Out
*Frostwatch, Day 4, Year 700AR — continued*

---

## Breaking Through

Back in the unclaimed reaches, **Bo-Bo** and **Alden** got to work on the weak spot in the
bricked-off tunnel Thistle had shown them, while **Ender** and **Vail** held either end of the
passage to watch their backs.

Ender was the first to notice it — a low growl, another Urco somewhere out there, barely
audible and never getting any closer. Nobody wanted to find out if that would change, so the
digging picked up its pace. Rushing it cost them a little — a loose brick came down on
Alden's head, nothing serious, and the work kept going until the opening was wide enough to
squeeze through.

---

## The Other Side

On the other side, the tunnels were a different world. Lanterns, well spaced and burning low,
meant somebody was keeping them fueled. The stone was old but patched and maintained, and the
dirt floor showed only light foot traffic. Across from where they came through, someone had
scratched a wave symbol into the wall.

They headed north first. A short side passage turned up a couple of crates full of loose gears
and gear-pins — Bo-Bo helped herself to a few — and another mark on the wall by the entrance,
this one a coil. She'd started keeping track of the symbols on her map, for whatever good that
would do, since none of these tunnels were actually on it.

Further north, a locked door stopped them cold — locked from the other side, which meant even
Bo-Bo couldn't get at it. Ender checked underneath: no light, but the air on the other side
felt damp.

They doubled back, passing the side tunnel and their own entry point, until they hit another
side passage on the right. This one ran deeper than their torch could reach, and nobody was in
a hurry to find out how deep. It had its own marking — a chalk circle next to another coil —
and they left it alone.

---

## The Crossing

Ahead, the main corridor ran straight for a couple hundred feet, with what looked like a
crossing passage about a hundred feet down. While they watched, a humanoid figure crossed it
in two quick strides, west to east, and was gone.

Ender led the chase. He kept the figure in sight long enough to clock a package tucked under
its arm, then lost it around a side corridor — right about the time he heard a door get
unlocked, opened, and shut again. They crept up to it anyway. Heard nothing.

Bo-Bo recognized the lock on sight — the same stubborn type that had beaten her before — and
gave it her best shot. Came closer than usual. Still didn't open.

So they went back to the crossing and decided to wait for the next courier instead. Bo-Bo took
the ceiling, cloaked and out of sight; the rest pressed flat against the west wall, out of the
sightline from the west corridor.

---

## The Courier

When the next one came through, Bo-Bo dropped on him — knocked a small chest out of his hands,
landed a little rough and cracked the wood doing it, scooped the chest up, and bolted north,
hoping he'd chase her instead of running the other way.

He froze for a beat, turned to run after Bo-Bo — and then saw the other three waiting for him
and stopped short, turning to run in the direction he had been originally going. Bo-Bo called
out to take him alive. **Alden** had other plans, and dropped him on the spot with Blighting
Strike.

The search turned up an address on a scrap of parchment, a handful of silver, and a strange
key on a tether around his neck. They dragged the body north out of the way while Bo-Bo opened
the chest — a matched set of jewelry, necklace, brooch, and earrings, all worked into the
symbol of **Noctis** in white, gray, and black moonstone. Nothing magic about it, as far as
they could tell. Noctis being an established deity didn't stop the table from immediately
suspecting a cult.

---

## A Very Polite Offer

Jewelry pocketed and the body propped against the wall like he'd dozed off mid-shift, they
started back toward the locked door — and ran straight into a problem. A man was standing in
the east corridor, sword on his hip, an insignia on his armband too far off to make out.
"These corridors are restricted," he told them. "I need to know who you are and how you got
here." The party tried the Adventurers' Guild line. He wasn't buying it.

Ender, never much for talking things out, put an arrow in him instead. The man staggered back
around the corner and out of sight, and the party followed anyway, Alden in front.

Around the corner were three more, armored, armed, and not drawing on anyone. The woman
leading them ran through the same questions — who they were, why they were here, how they'd
gotten in — and reminded them, patiently, that the Guild had no authority down here. The party
kept insisting otherwise.

Eventually she gave up trying to reason with them. *"Look, let's just escort you out of the
corridors and back to the streets above and this will all be done with,"* she said. *"There is
no need for further violence and bloodshed."* The party disagreed. Ender put arrows into three
of them before the group broke and scattered down the side tunnels. Nobody chased. They just
wanted that door.

---

## The Key

The key from the courier worked. It opened onto a tunnel sloping up thirty feet to a second
locked door, and the same key handled that one too. They came out into the upper sewers with
daylight leaking down through openings to the north and south — late afternoon by then, pushing
4:30 or 5:00. They took the north opening and surfaced around the corner from Saltstone
Square.

From there, straight to the Dockworkers' Guildhall to report in to **Dorna**.

---

## Notes

- **The tunnels beyond the weak spot** — maintained, lit by low-burning lanterns, and lightly
traveled. Three wall markings were noted and added to Bo-Bo's map: a wave, a coil, and a chalk
circle beside a second coil.
- **The couriers** — at least two people crossed the main corridor carrying packages. The first
slipped away with his package; the second, carrying a small locked chest, was killed in the
crossing.
- **The courier's belongings** — a scrap of parchment with an address, a handful of silver, a
strange key on a tether (it opens two locked doors leading up to the upper sewers), and a
chest holding a matched set of Noctis-symbol jewelry in white, gray, and black moonstone. The
jewelry is not magical, as far as the party can tell.
- **The restricted corridors** — an armed group in these tunnels insisted the party leave and
dismissed the Adventurers' Guild's authority here. Several of them were shot and the rest
scattered. Their insignia was not identified.
- Another **Urco** was heard in the unclaimed reaches, close enough to growl but never closer.
- Bo-Bo took a few gears and gear-pins from crates in the north side passage.
