# Session 07 — A Handful of Gears, a Collector of Oddities, and a Library After Hours
*Frostwatch, Day 4, Year 700AR — continued*

---

## Reporting In

The Dockworkers' Guildhall sent the party straight into **Dorna Bitterbuckle's** office, and the
meeting went downhill from there.

**Vail** asked whether Dorna knew anyone named Thistle, or had ever heard of an Urco. She
hadn't, not on either count. An Ahuizotl, on the other hand, she recognized right away, which
did nothing for her mood. As far as Dorna could tell, none of this had anything to do with the
job she'd hired them for, and she said so.

If they wanted her attention, she told them, bring her something pertinent.

**Bo-Bo** emptied a handful of small gears and gear-pins onto the desk.

Dorna looked at the little pile. She looked at Bo-Bo. She wanted to know what, exactly, a
scatter of loose parts had to do with repairs that had now failed to hold three times at four
separate locations. There was no good answer to that, and nobody tried very hard to give one.

**Alden** took a different tack and told her about the guards: how they'd said the party had no
business or permission in those tunnels, how the party had insisted the Adventurers' Guild had
cleared them, and how the guards had answered that the Guild had no jurisdiction down there.
He'd also drawn the symbol from the armband one of them wore, and he showed her the sketch.
Dorna flinched. It was barely there, a hair's breadth of a reaction, but Alden caught it.

---

## Two More Hands

Rather than press the point, Dorna had hired more help. She brought in **Rorik Ironvow**, a
dwarf Seraph, and **Silas Stonebreaker**, an Earthkin Druid, and introduced them as the newest
members of the team. The hope was plain enough: with two more sets of hands, the party might
come back the following evening with real progress on the job instead of a pile of random parts
and a collection of odd stories.

---

## The Most Immediate Danger

The party left the guildhall and headed back to the Strangled Goat for some refreshment. Before
she even sat down, **Vail** reached out with her Liminal Awareness and asked it a simple
question: *what is the most immediate danger here?*

Her senses barely pointed at an older, scholarly-looking man sitting at the party's usual table.

Vail walked over and started dancing on the table. He offered her a seat. She took her time
about it, but she eventually took it. **Bo-Bo** skipped the table entirely and settled into the
rafters to watch from above.

The rest of the party soon joined them, and the man introduced himself as **Oren**. He was an
academic of a sort, though more a collector of information and oddities than a scholar. He
answered to no one, he said, and wanted nothing from the collecting but the collecting itself.
Notoriety held no interest for him.

He'd also heard the party had been in the sewers recently, and he wanted to know what they'd
found down there. The party gave him nothing. Oren didn't seem put out. If they'd tell him what
they knew and what they'd found, he said, he would try to get them a map of the lower sewer
system.

---

## After Hours at the Library

**Rorik** had a better idea: break into the Bridgeport Library and look for maps. Vail didn't
even hesitate and headed for the door. Oren's only addition was where to look: the maps section
first, he suggested, and if that turned up nothing, the restricted section.

At the library, **Bo-Bo** made a circuit of the building on her Midnight Spirit, pirate hat and
all, looking for a way in. She settled on the service entrance at the back, and the party
followed her around.

Bo-Bo picked the lock and they slipped inside. Rorik bumped into her on the way through the
door, which turned out to be the luckiest thing that happened all night. She'd never checked the
floor, and the bump kept her from stepping on an alarm plate.

Inside, the maps section gave them plans for the first level down and nothing more. Bo-Bo picked
the lock on the gate to the restricted section, then set about making drawings of the maps they
had already found while the others dug through the piles.

The restricted section was scrolls, parchment, and tomes stacked with no apparent order, and
most of it made no sense. Then someone found a scrap that mentioned the name **Keshari**, said
that they had *left Orb*, and referred to the bright moon, **Lustre**. The parchment was in poor
shape and the rest was unreadable, made no sense, or didn't provide any potentially useful
information.

They still have no map.
