# Dorian Voss

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Greenhook Exchange, Trellen Gap*

---

A composed human member of the Merchants' Exchange, Dorian operates the Greenhook Exchange, a
trade house and brokerage that moves goods, contracts, and coin through Trellen Gap —
particularly timber, connecting the hamlet's output to broader markets it couldn't otherwise
reach. His ledgers are immaculate, his contracts fair, and everything about the operation
reads as orderly and entirely legitimate.

Meticulous and aloof but courteous, Dorian rarely shows emotion past polite professionalism,
and seems to know more than he openly shares — aware of shipments before they arrive, of
supply shifts before they happen, with an accuracy that invites both trust and quiet scrutiny.
Whether that comes from an exceptionally disciplined mind, an unusually good network of
contacts, or something less transparent, he offers no indication either way, and honors every
agreement he makes regardless.
