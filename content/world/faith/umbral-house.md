# Umbral House

A moody, slate-roofed shrine on Bridgeport's northern outskirts, dedicated to [Noctis]({{< relref "the-gods#noctis" >}}). 
Its narrow windows and dim interior give the building an unsettling air, and about as many 
people avoid it as dare step inside. Locals talk of strange chills, flickering lights, or 
the sense of being watched near its grounds — nothing anyone can prove, but nothing anyone 
seems eager to test either. Some claim that at twilight, silhouettes move through the halls 
even when the priests insist the shrine is empty.

## Why People Come Anyway

Those who do visit come seeking things other temples aren't well suited to address — 
protection from unseen dangers, clarity in the middle of deception, safe passage through 
darkness both physical and emotional, or relief from dreams that won't leave them alone. 
Travelers often arrive at dusk, hoping the shrine's quiet holds some steadying effect on 
whatever's following them.

## The Ceremonies

The priests keep things understated — soft voices, lanterns shaded in grey-blue glass. 
Offerings are simple: a candle left to burn low, a written worry slipped beneath an 
obsidian bowl, a silver coin set on the threshold stone. The shrine keeps a vellum ledger of 
these offerings, and locals swear it never fills — as if the shadow itself keeps turning the 
page.

Unsettling as it is, the Umbral House holds a real pull for anyone standing at a crossroads, 
or chasing a truth that daylight isn't going to hand them.
