# Thaleon Brijjar

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Thaleon directly*  
*Usually found: Carpenters' & Shipwrights' Guild hall, Drydock Quay*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Carpenters' & Shipwrights' Guild. A master shipwright whose
stern gaze and silvered beard are as recognizable as the vessels his guild produces.
Reserved and uncompromising, he carries himself with the precision of a man who has spent
a lifetime measuring twice and cutting once.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Thaleon's reputation is common knowledge among anyone connected to
Bridgeport's shipbuilding trade, but the party has had no direct dealings with him.

## Reputation

Known for absolute refusal to cut corners — on safety, on materials, on standards.
Apprentices claim he can spot a flawed joint from across a shipyard, and rival builders
quietly admit that a design bearing his mark is as good as a guarantee. When pressured to
rush construction for political or commercial convenience, Thaleon pushes back with the
immovable certainty of bedrock. His decisions may slow projects. They save lives.
