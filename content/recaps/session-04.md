# Session 04 — A Backwards Map, a River of Rats, and Something That Deflates When You Kill It
*Frostwatch, Day 4, Year 700AG*

---

## Dorna's Map

Morning came, and it was back to the Dockworkers' Guildhall to meet **Dorna Bitterbuckle**,
hoping this would finally be the day they got real access to the lower sewers instead of
another locked door. She made good on it — producing an old, badly incomplete map. According
to it, the closest access point to the west end of the Freehold sat inside a mausoleum in the
Godsfall Necropolis.

**Bo-Bo** copied down what she could of the map, but somewhere in the process flipped east and
west without noticing. Nobody caught the mistake until they were already underground.

---

## Into the Lower Sewers

The trip to the necropolis went smoothly. They found the mausoleum, picked the lock without
much trouble, found a grate set into the floor, pulled it up, dropped a rope, and made their
way down into the lower sewers.

**Vail** wanted a lit torch instead of Bo-Bo's spectral companion doing the honors — said she
needed something real down there in case of rats. Nobody argued the logic once they were
actually in the tunnels.

These tunnels were older, drier, and tighter than anything they'd been through before — barely
five feet across and never much more than seven or eight feet high. Past the edge of the
torchlight, nothing but dark and the sound of their own footsteps.

Working off the map — the backwards one, which by then they'd already caught onto, so at least
they weren't actually lost — they hit a caved-in stretch that would've been their best route
west. One look at the size of the blockage told them digging through wasn't happening, so they
found another way around and kept pushing.

---

## The River of Rats

**Alden** heard it first — squeaking, claws ticking against stone. Then, around a corner
they'd passed a few minutes earlier, a river of rats came pouring through. It didn't feel like
an attack. It felt like something back there had scared them into running. Vail bolted, the
rest followed her lead. They broke left at a Y-junction; the rats broke right.

They waited it out, catching their breath, listening for anything else coming. Nothing did.
Once it felt safe, they backtracked to check the tunnel the rats had come pouring out of — and
felt, more than heard, a low growl somewhere in the dark ahead of them. Something moved toward
them, slow, and then stepped into the light.

An undead-looking hound plodded toward them, though it turned out to be nothing of the sort.
Whatever it was, it barely had a chance to put up a fight — but when they went to check the
body, it deflated right in front of them, losing almost all its mass in a few seconds, as if
there'd never been much solid to it in the first place. They rolled what was left in a spare
cloak. Vail kept it, said she wanted to look at it properly when there was time.

They kept moving. Somewhere along the way, part of the tunnel gave out overhead — **Alden**
got caught in the collapse, came away banged up but fine, and the others dug him free.

Not long after, they felt that same low growl again — another of whatever they'd just put
down. Vail wanted to go after it. The rest of them, knowing exactly how that goes, went along
anyway. Left, right, left again, and they never so much as caught sight of it. Eventually they
gave up the chase.

---

## Something in the Water

By then they'd angled north, toward the Godsfall River, and everything had gotten wetter —
puddles, standing water, the kind of damp that gets into your boots. One puddle was easy
enough to step around. The next one looked the same, right up until Vail stepped in it and
something with a clawed, prehensile tail came out of the water after her. It missed her, and
they put it down fast — too large and too strange to belong in a sewer, more the kind of thing
that's supposed to live along the river.

This one didn't deflate. Too big to carry off, too big to do anything with. Past where it had
been hiding, the tunnel sloped down and looked like it went fully underwater, though they'd
need to get closer to know for sure.

That's where the night ended.

---

## Notes

- The route to the west end of the Freehold runs through a mausoleum in the Godsfall
Necropolis, down into tunnels older and tighter than anything the party has explored so far.
- Two creatures have now been put down in these tunnels: one that deflated to almost nothing
after death, and one with a clawed, prehensile tail that came out of standing water. Neither
has been identified.
- A third of whatever the first two were is still down there somewhere — the party gave up
the chase without catching sight of it.
- The tunnel ahead slopes down into what looks like a fully flooded stretch. Unconfirmed how
far it goes or whether it opens back up.
