# Council of Trades — Members

The Council of Trades is drawn from Bridgeport's ten most influential guilds and 
professional orders. Each guild elects a **Guildmaster-Delegate** to serve a five-year 
term. Together they govern the Freehold, with one member serving as **High Convenor** — 
currently **Seraphina Velcross** of the Merchants' Exchange.
