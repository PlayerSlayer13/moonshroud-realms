# Marelin Quillspire

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Marelin directly*  
*Usually found: Scriptorium Society hall*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Scriptorium Society. Composed, precise, and possessed of a
memory that unsettles people who underestimate it. She can recall details from
decades-old texts and is frequently consulted by the Council when legal precedent or
historical context must be established.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Marelin's reputation is common knowledge among anyone connected to
Bridgeport's scholarly and legal circles, but the party has had no direct dealings with
her.

## Reputation

Her chief concern is safeguarding truth — preserving it, protecting it, and ensuring it
remains accessible. She pushes for expanded archival funding, improved literacy programs,
and stricter regulations on falsified documents. Regarded as one of the Council's
steadiest voices, and the member least likely to say anything she doesn't mean.
