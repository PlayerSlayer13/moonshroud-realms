# Thistle

*First encountered: A junction chamber in the lower sewers beneath Bridgeport, Frostwatch Day 4, 700AG*  
*Usually found: Unknown — encountered once, deep beneath the city*

---

A Fae observer encountered behind an unmarked door in a five-way junction deep in the sewers
beneath Bridgeport, candlelight flickering from whatever space he's made for himself down
there. Polite, unhurried, and plainly at home in a place most people would find deeply
uncomfortable to linger in.

## What the Party Knows

Thistle described himself as an observer who has watched over "the unclaimed reaches" beneath
the city for longer than he cared to count. He knew the name of a creature the party had
killed earlier that day without being told — an Urco — and in the course of conversation named
an organization the party had never heard of before: the Fernoir.

He deals in bargains rather than straight answers. In exchange for an account of how the Urco
was killed, he agreed to guide the party to a weak spot in one of the Fernoir's sealed
boundaries. A second bargain — information about why the Fernoir walls off certain tunnels —
cost Vail a name of personal significance, one she won't be able to speak again until she's
back on the surface.

## Impressions

Genuinely warm rather than merely polite, and visibly pleased when Vail told him she'd come
visit again. Nothing about him reads as dangerous, but nothing about him reads as fully
explained either — he seems to know more than he offers, and to offer exactly as much as suits
him.
