# Session 02 — A Locked Door Nobody Asked For, and the First Real Certification Failure
*Harvestide, Day 23, Year 700AG — continued*

---

## The Door Beneath the Hideout

With Rask handled and his crew cleared out, the party swept the rest of
the hideout before calling it done. That's when they found it — a
stretch of wall that didn't match the rest of the sewer construction,
older, rougher, like it had been standing there long before anyone
thought to build a sewer around it. Set into it was a door that looked
just as wrong: too well-made for the tunnels it sat in, locked with a
mechanism none of them had seen before.

**Bo-Bo** took a run at it. The lock didn't cooperate — one of her picks
jammed hard enough to send a loud clunk echoing through the door and the
stone around it. Everybody froze. There was a beat where it felt like
something out there had noticed, some pressure in the air that hadn't
been there a second before. It passed. Nobody wanted to say out loud
what they'd all just felt, so nobody did.

She went back at it once the moment passed, and this time the lock
gave.

Behind it: a short, dark corridor, and at the end of it, another door —
a better lock this time, one nobody at the table had the skill to
touch. **Vail** reached for that same sense of wrongness she'd felt
earlier in the session, and got it again, sharper now, with a clearer
read on which direction it was coming from.

---

## Loose Ends at the Goat

That was enough for one dungeon. The party backtracked to **Fen**,
settled up, and got paid for cleaning out his stock thieves. On the way
out, somebody clocked that the pirate captain and his crew weren't at
their usual table in the Goat anymore. Filed away. Doesn't mean
anything yet.

---

## The Racket District

Vail wasn't done chasing the wrongness. She wanted a better fix on it
than she could get underground, so she took it topside. Ender went
with her — less out of curiosity, more because somebody should probably
keep an eye on things. Bo-Bo and Alden stayed at the Goat and drank,
which, all things considered, was probably the correct call.

Up top, Vail's read narrowed to a specific stretch of the Racket —
warehouses and boarding houses clustered around one enormous,
unreasonably healthy tree, the kind of tree that has no business
looking that good in a district like this. Mostly dockworkers living
there, people who keep odd hours and don't ask a lot of questions.

They found two of them on a back set of steps, working through a jug of
ale — Jaylen and Dusker. Asked about the tree first. Got a shrug. Just
a tree. Always been there. Asked if anything strange had been going on
lately, and that's where it got interesting. One of them mentioned the
road down by the docks — the one that keeps sinking no matter how many
times the city fills it back in. The other passed along something
secondhand, a friend-of-a-friend story about someone who'd been walking
through the district, realized they weren't where they thought they
should be, turned around, and just... came back. No further detail.
That's how rumors work.

Vail went and looked at the sinking road herself. Nothing revelatory —
it's doing exactly what it's apparently always done, which is sink.

Night wound down. In the morning, it was Guild business.

*Harvestide, Day 24, Year 700AG*

## Certification Day

Adventurers' Guild certification: paperwork, then the trials —
physical, combat, written — each a chance to embarrass yourself in
front of strangers holding clipboards. Everybody cleared the lot except
Bo-Bo, who went down on the physical. Not the end of the world — she'll
get another shot — but for someone who spent four years running a
pirate deck, it stung a little more than she let on.

- Two open threads from the Racket District: a road near the docks

who briefly lost their bearings in the district and simply "came back."
- The party has advanced in experience following the Rask encounter.
