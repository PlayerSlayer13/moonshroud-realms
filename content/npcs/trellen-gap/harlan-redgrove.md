# Harlan Redgrove

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Redgrove Millworks, Trellen Gap*

---

A broad-shouldered human affiliated with the Carpenters' & Shipwrights' Guild, Harlan owns and
operates Redgrove Millworks, the primary processing site for timber cut anywhere near Trellen
Gap. Nearly every log felled within reach of the hamlet passes through his yard, making the
mill a central pillar of the local economy. He maintains close working ties with timber camps
like Duenna Whitwood's, coordinating laborers and saw crews to keep supply and output in
balance.

Practical and measured, Harlan focuses on what works rather than what sounds good, rarely
rushing a decision and expecting hard work in return for fair treatment. Not outwardly
political, but not naive either — he understands that the flow of timber through his mill
affects trade, wages, and the stability of the whole settlement, and prioritizes keeping his
operation running smoothly and his workers paid over anything grander.
