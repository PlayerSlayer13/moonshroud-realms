# Erlan Fennoren

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Erlan directly*  
*Usually found: Dancing Ghoul Inn, near the Godsfall Necropolis*

---

Halfling proprietor of the Dancing Ghoul Inn, a lively establishment near the edge of the
Godsfall Necropolis. Jovial and heavyset, with an easy laugh and a habit of personally
tending the bar despite owning the place outright.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. The Dancing Ghoul is a well-regarded stop for food, drink, and local
gossip, but the party hasn't been through its doors.

## Reputation

Good-humored, welcoming, and fond of increasingly ridiculous ghost stories about the inn's
name and origins — entertaining rather than believable, though he keeps a sharp memory for
names, debts, and trouble brewing before it fully starts.
