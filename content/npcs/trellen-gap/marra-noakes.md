# Marra Noakes

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Great Forest Sundry, Trellen Gap*

---

A halfling member of the Merchants' Exchange, Marra owns and runs the Great Forest Sundry, one
of Trellen Gap's essential supply points — rope, lanterns, trail rations, tools, and travel
necessities for locals and passing adventurers alike. Age has slowed her physically, but her
sense of the hamlet's rhythms hasn't dulled at all: she notices who's preparing for a longer
journey, who's buying more than usual, and when patterns shift.

Observant and soft-spoken but firm, Marra plans ahead rather than guessing, and shortages are
rare under her watch. Profit matters to her, but reliability matters more — a missed supply or
faulty item can ripple outward in a place as isolated as Trellen Gap, and she takes care not to
be the cause. Purchasing patterns and unusual requests are often the first sign she has that
something in the hamlet is amiss.
