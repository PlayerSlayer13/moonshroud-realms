# Gullfolk

Gullfolk are a feathered coastal people, humanoid in build but unmistakably avian in
bearing. They are sharp-eyed and quick to tilt their heads at anything moving on the
horizon, and they are comfortable balanced on a mast rail or lighthouse ledge where no one
else would stand easily.

---

## In the World

### Appearance

Gullfolk are lean and sharp-featured, with beaks and feathered crests that run along the
scalp and forearms in shades of grey, white, and slate. Their plumage is often streaked by
salt wind and age, and their voices carry something of the sharp, far-reaching calls of the
birds they descend from. Individuals vary, from lean and
angular to broad and heavyset.

### Origins

Gullfolk are descended from the seabirds that once nested along Algar's western shoals.
They trace their lineage to the founding fishing community that grew, centuries later,
into Pistil Point.

### Character and Culture

Gullfolk take naturally to maritime work: fishing, piloting, lighthouse-keeping, and harbor
watch. Their communities value patience with the tide over haste with the day. In Pistil
Point, gullfolk families run several of the village's businesses, and many of the
volunteer watchmen at the village gates are retired gullfolk mariners. Gullfolk have also been seen
working as tavern staff and publicans, quick-moving and sharp-eyed.

## Playing a Gullfolk

*The following is for players creating a Gullfolk character using the Daggerheart system.*

### Ancestry Features

**Keen Eyes**
You have Advantage on Instinct Rolls made to spot something at a great distance, notice
movement on open water, or pick a familiar face out of a crowd.

**Riding the Wind**
You can glide on rising air currents. Treat any fall as though it were half the actual
distance, and you take no damage from falling alone. Once per rest, you may spend a Hope to
glide up to Close range horizontally as you descend, landing wherever you choose within
that range.
