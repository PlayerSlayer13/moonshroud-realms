# Aruush Ghoro

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Aruush directly*  
*Usually found: The Brass Axiom*

---

Human hierophant of the Brass Axiom, Bridgeport's temple to Absot. A thoughtful and deeply
respected presence whose calm judgment has made him one of the more trusted voices in the
city, though he speaks only when he believes his words will add value.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Aruush's counsel is sought by surveyors, magistrates, and civic officials
dealing with matters of consequence, but the party hasn't crossed his path.

## Reputation

Calm, observant, and difficult to rush — he listens far more than he speaks, and often
communicates through brief written notes or trusted subordinates rather than direct address.
When he does choose to speak plainly, both his clergy and visitors treat it as worth careful
consideration.
