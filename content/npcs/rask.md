# Rask

*First encountered: Beneath the Strangled Goat, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*  
*Status: Deceased*

---

A wererat operating beneath the Strangled Goat — the architect of the 
missing stock operation that had been draining Fen Whistling Man's 
inventory for some time. Rask ran a small network of muscle from a 
makeshift base in the sewer tunnels below Saltstone Square, redirecting 
stolen goods through channels that suited him.

## What the Party Knows

Rask stepped from behind an old sheet in his sewer room and attempted 
to negotiate — offering to bring the party in, pay them off, or 
otherwise make them someone else's problem. They declined.

**Bo-Bo** gave him a shallow cut on the arm as a warning. He hadn't 
reached for a weapon, so the others held. Then he transformed — 
wererat — and bolted for one of his bolt holes.

**Axis** caught him in the tunnel. Rask wriggled free. Bo-Bo scooped 
him up. **Ender** ended it.

*"Figures. Someone always wants to end a good thing."*

His room contained approximately 30 gold pieces, a ledger documenting 
the stolen goods operation, personal effects, and an old oilskin 
containing two items of unknown significance.

## Notes

Rask was clearly not working alone — the muscle in the adjacent room 
suggests an operation with more backing than a single wererat in a 
sewer. Whether anyone above him is aware the operation has ended 
remains to be seen.
