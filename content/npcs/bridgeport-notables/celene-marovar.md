# Celene Marovar

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Celene directly*  
*Usually found: Temple of Veyruun*

---

Human Prior of the Temple of Veyruun, overseeing one of Bridgeport's most influential
religious and financial institutions. Supervises currency exchanges, secure vaults, letters
of credit, and charitable loans alongside the temple's more conventional religious affairs.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Celene's influence over Bridgeport's merchant class is well known, but the
party hasn't had dealings with her.

## Reputation

Measured, perceptive, and difficult to surprise. Rather than asking what something is worth,
she's more likely to ask what it might become. Merchants, guildmasters, and investors who
demonstrate competence and sound judgment tend to find few allies in Bridgeport more
influential than her.
