# Elira Vanneth

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Reeve's Manor, Trellen Gap*

---

An even-tempered aelfaen appointed by the Duchy of Rhundel to serve as reeve of Trellen Gap,
responsible for order, trade and taxation, and keeping the hamlet productive and secure. She
acts as the primary liaison between the Crown and the local guilds, particularly those tied to
timber and transport, and takes particular care to respect local customs and long-standing
warnings — especially concerning the nearby Aelthrane ruins — even when caution conflicts with
opportunity.

Even-handed and quietly authoritative, Elira listens before she speaks and rarely raises her
voice, though her word carries real weight in the hamlet. She prefers diplomacy but isn't
passive: when a problem exceeds what the militia or townsfolk can manage, she quietly seeks
capable outside help rather than draw the Crown's full attention to Trellen Gap's business.
