# Fen Whistling Man

*First encountered: The Strangled Goat, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*  
*Usually found: The Strangled Goat, Saltstone Square*

---

Barkeep at the **Strangled Goat** in Saltstone Square. A Tropical 
Ursinor with striking panda-like markings — bold black and white 
coloring that makes him immediately recognizable in the Goat's 
busy common room.

## What the Party Knows

Fen struck up a conversation with Vail and Ender at the bar — 
casual on its surface, careful underneath. He was assessing them: 
who they were, who they worked for, whether they could be trusted 
with a problem that needed handling quietly.

Satisfied that they were what they appeared to be — a group recently 
come together, not affiliated with any guild or guild-adjacent 
organization — he asked for their help. Stock had been going missing 
from the Goat in quantities that couldn't be explained by ordinary 
loss. He needed it found and stopped by people who wouldn't report 
back to anyone official.

His offer: enough to cover the 25GP Adventurer's Guild certification 
fee for each member of the party.

He showed them the storeroom beneath the Goat and the sewer entrance 
hidden behind a stack of goods. He provided two lanterns. He told them 
to knock on the trap door when they returned — he would keep it latched 
but would be listening.

## Impressions

Measured. Pragmatic. He sized the party up before saying a word about 
his problem, and only approached when he was reasonably confident in 
what he was dealing with. He's not a man who takes unnecessary risks 
with his employer's business or his own.

The panda markings are hard to miss. The careful eyes behind them are 
worth noting.
