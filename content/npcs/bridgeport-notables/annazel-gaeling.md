# Annazel Gaeling

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Annazel directly*  
*Usually found: Shrine of the Golden Harvest*

---

Human prior of the Shrine of the Golden Harvest, devoted to Barrowen Tallgrain. Serves as
both religious leader and community organizer for Bridgeport's eastern farming district,
overseeing charitable works, harvest relief, and agricultural outreach alongside her
spiritual duties.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Annazel's role at the shrine and her reputation for practical charity are
common knowledge among the farming communities east of the city, but the party hasn't met
her.

## Reputation

Warm and approachable, treating farmers, merchants, and nobles with the same courtesy.
Patient, organized, and rarely rattled, with an extensive network of contacts throughout the
Godsfall Valley — the kind of person who tends to know who can help when a problem outgrows
the shrine's own reach.
