# Melodari

*Third-party and homebrew ancestry, compatible with the Moonshroud Realms. Not Soulstone canon.*

Melodari are the rare descendants of sirens and humanoid parents. Their voices carry an
innate, otherworldly melody, and their features often hint at an oceanic heritage:
iridescent skin, faint scales, flowing hair, and subtly webbed fingers.

---

## In the World

### Origins

Sirens were ancient fey or ocean-bound spirits whose voices could calm storms and lure
fleets astray. A Melodari's siren ancestry is distant, but the legacy is unmistakable.
Their voices carry the resonance of old currents, and their presence stirs emotion as
naturally as the moon stirs the tides.

### Character and Culture

Drawn to music, diplomacy, and the sea, Melodari often become bards, envoys, and
lore-keepers who bridge the worlds of land and water. They are rare.

---

## Playing a Melodari

*The following is for players creating a Melodari character using the Daggerheart system.*

### Ancestry Features

**Siren's Blood**
You can breathe air and water, and you have Advantage on Agility Rolls made while
swimming.

**Siren's Legacy**
Once per rest, you may mark a Stress to let your voice carry an enchanting resonance.
Target a creature within Close range that can hear you. That creature must make an Instinct
Reaction Roll against your Presence. On a failure, they are Vulnerable and cannot willingly
move away from you until the end of your next action. On a success, the effect fades
immediately. This ability has no effect on creatures that are immune to being charmed or
that cannot hear.
