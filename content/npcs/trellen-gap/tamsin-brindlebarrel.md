# Tamsin Brindlebarrel

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Clumsy Apple Inn, Trellen Gap*

---

A halfling loosely affiliated with the Hospitality Guild, Tamsin runs the Clumsy Apple Inn,
Trellen Gap's primary lodging and gathering place — usually the first stop for travelers,
merchants, and adventurers passing through, and she makes sure it leaves a good impression.
Beyond meals and rooms, she acts as an informal hub of information, collecting the stories and
small concerns that pass through her common room and quietly sorting what's worth repeating
from what isn't.

Warm but watchful, Tamsin greets every guest with a smile and misses very little in the
process, keeping the inn running smoothly even at its busiest. She has little patience for
troublemakers and won't hesitate to call in the militia if a situation calls for it, though she
much prefers to defuse things herself before they escalate that far.
