# Vaelora Thistlemere

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Vaelora directly*  
*Usually found: Temple of the Verdant Court*

---

Aelfaen prior of the Temple of the Verdant Court and a former wandering druid before entering
Charr's priesthood. Oversees one of Bridgeport's largest religious institutions, coordinating
worship, charitable works, and community programs across all four members of the Verdant
Court.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. The party hasn't met Vaelora.

## Reputation

Patient, thoughtful, and quietly wise, rarely rushing to judgment. Regarded as skilled at
finding common purpose among people with different priorities — to her, stewardship, family,
and wilderness are all part of the same living whole.
