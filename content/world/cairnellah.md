# Cairnellah

Cairnellah is a walled royal port of nearly three thousand souls, standing where the Sea of Zephyrys meets Godsfall Bay on the western coast of Algar. It is a working harbor first, with streets shaped as much by trade and tide as by the age of dragons that left its oldest foundations. Humans, mustelins, aelfaen, and halflings make up most of the population, with visible communities of dwarves, gnomes, ursinor, dragonborn, dragonblood, gullfolk, and others drawn by its sea and sky routes.

## The Port and the Fishery

A line of stone wharfs and wooden piers runs along the whole western face of the town, where fishing boats land their catch and coastal traders come and go with the tide. The fishery is large for a town this size and feeds both local demand and the caravans that carry salted fish inland. The shipyards are known for sturdy ketches and, on commission, two-masted schooners, and they do a steady trade in repairs for ships too damaged to reach the bigger yards at Bridgeport or Annopore.

## The Bimbot Lands and the Sky

Cairnellah lies roughly ninety miles east of the gnomish island nation of the Bimbot Lands and is the closest Algarian port to it. Most of that trade still passes through Bridgeport and Annopore, but a steady stream of goods reaches Cairnellah overland before making the shorter crossing west. An airship landing field inside the walls strengthens the link and draws gnomish merchants and travelers who prefer the sky routes.

## The Crown and the Castle

The royal compound takes up nearly a sixth of the walled town, and Cairn Castle stands in its northwest corner, looking out over the sea, the bay, and the lighthouse on the skerries offshore. The barony's seat is now at Sand Manor, fifty miles to the south, but the castle remains the historical seat of power. Baron Sand holds lordship over it and returns when matters require. In his absence a seneschal represents the Crown, and the compound's halls, barracks, and stables make it very nearly a settlement of its own.

## Older Foundations

Cairnellah traces its beginnings to an age of dragon rule, and some of that age survives in the town. A restored great hall was once part of a dragon palace, and the immense gate that great wyrms once flew through is now the anchorage for airships, put to new use but not forgotten.

## The Land Around

Windmills turn across the eastern fields, powering the farms and mills that stretch toward Bridgeport and the edge of the Wexwood Forest. To the south the land is harsher, with white sands, coarse grass, and driftwood along a windswept coast. Inside the walls, temples to many deities stand among the homes and markets, and the town watches the horizon for whatever the next tide brings.
