# Deldar Navalur

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Shrine of the Luminous Birch, Trellen Gap*

---

A young aelfaen deacon in service to Luaur, Deldar tends the Shrine of the Luminous Birch with
quiet devotion — this is his first independent posting, assigned after years of study in the
ducal seat of Rhundel, and now approaching his third year in the Gap, he considers the hamlet
home. His duties extend well past formal ritual: counseling laborers, mediating small
disputes, and offering comfort in hardship as much as tending the shrine itself.

Earnest and measured in speech, Deldar approaches his role with genuine sincerity rather than
performed piety, humbly confident in his training while staying open to learning past it. His
regular visits to Vaelis Thornebloom have shaped his understanding of faith as much as any
formal doctrine, and he's especially attentive to anything that might disturb the balance
around Trellen Gap — including quiet concerns tied to the nearby Aelthrane ruins, which he
doesn't speak of lightly.
