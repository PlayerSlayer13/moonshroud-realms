# Rella Coppins

*First encountered: Copper Lane, Frostwatch Day 3, 700AG*  
*Usually found: Copper Lane*

---

A city infrastructure maintenance worker who has spent the past six weeks surveying Copper
Lane's recurring sinkhole — the same stretch of road that keeps sinking no matter how many
times the city fills it back in. Methodical and precise, with the patience of someone who's
used to nobody believing a problem is as strange as she knows it to be.

## What the Party Knows

Rella handed over her unofficial survey notes on the sinking road when the party arrived
under contract to investigate it, and pointed out a nearby sewer grate as an unused way down
that nobody had bothered to check. That lead took the party underground, where they found the
first of the strange locked doors tied to Dorna Bitterbuckle's contract.

## Impressions

Practical and thorough, more interested in getting an honest answer than in being right about
her own theories. Gave the impression of someone who's been quietly documenting something odd
for weeks with no one else taking it seriously.
