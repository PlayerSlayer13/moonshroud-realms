# Seela & Noori Brookcurrent

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met the Brookcurrents directly*  
*Usually found: Riverview Inn*

---

Twin siblings who co-run the Riverview Inn, a lively riverside establishment popular with
river captains, sailors, and travelers moving upriver. Seela is the more outgoing of the two,
quick to welcome newcomers and swap stories; Noori handles supplies, accounts, and the
practical details that keep a busy inn running. Neither is truly in charge — the place runs
on the two of them working in tandem.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. The party hasn't stayed at the Riverview.

## Reputation

Friendly, practical, and protective of their guests and staff, with deep ties throughout the
Godsfall Valley's river community — often among the first to hear news arriving from upriver
settlements.
