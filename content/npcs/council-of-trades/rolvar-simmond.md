# Rolvar Simmond

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Rolvar directly*  
*Usually found: Brewers' & Grocers' Compact hall, Rivergate Market*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Brewers' & Grocers' Compact. Broad-shouldered, steady-eyed,
and has spent decades rooting out fraudulent merchants, watered ale, and spoiled grain
with an accuracy that has become something of a legend in the Compact. Known for fairness
and good humor, greeting workers by name and sharing stories earned from a lifetime on
the trade floor.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Rolvar's reputation is common knowledge among anyone connected to
Bridgeport's food and drink trade, but the party has had no direct dealings with him.

## Reputation

Driven by a simple conviction: the people of Bridgeport deserve honest food and clean
drink. In emergencies he is decisive and practical, deploying reserves and rerouting
caravans before most people realize there is a problem. One of the less politically
visible members of the Council, which he seems to prefer.
