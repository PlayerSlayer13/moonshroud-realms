# Dhampir

*Third-party and homebrew ancestry, compatible with the Moonshroud Realms. Not Soulstone canon.*

Dhampir are the rare offspring of vampires and humanoids, carrying mortal vitality
alongside an undead hunger. They mature at the same rate as humans, but their lifespans
stretch to centuries under the lingering influence of their vampiric blood.

---

## In the World

### Character and Culture

Most dhampir walk the line between light and shadow. Some are haunted by their hunger, and
others master it in order to protect the people who fear them. Every dhampir feels the pull
of the thirst, a whisper beneath the heartbeat and a reminder of what they could become.
Many choose to master it anyway, as proof that a creature born of darkness can still choose
the light.

---

## Playing a Dhampir

*The following is for players creating a Dhampir character using the Daggerheart system.*

### Ancestry Features

**Undead Resilience**
You have Advantage on Agility and Strength Rolls made to resist or endure the effects of
poison, disease, or similar physical afflictions. Once per rest, when you would mark a Hit
Point from magic damage, you may mark a Stress instead.

**Dark Thirst**
Once per rest, when you are within Melee range of a creature that is Restrained or
Vulnerable and that creature has blood, you may mark a Stress to bite them. Make a Finesse
or Instinct attack roll against that creature. On a success, they mark a Hit Point and you
clear a Hit Point. This ability has no effect on constructs or the undead.
