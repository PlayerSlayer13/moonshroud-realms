# Bruna Emberfall

*First encountered: The Strangled Goat, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*  
*Usually found: The Strangled Goat, Saltstone Square*

---

Owner of the **Strangled Goat** tavern in Saltstone Square. Bruna 
Emberfall runs one of Bridgeport's most reliably busy establishments 
with the quiet authority of someone who has been doing this long enough 
to know exactly when to intervene and exactly how.

## What the Party Knows

Bruna stepped to the party's table during the evening and offered her 
hand to Vail — a diplomatic signal that the table dancing portion of 
the night might reasonably conclude. The ship's captain in the corner 
had caught her eye with a look that said, plainly, *please end this.* 
She handled it without making a scene.

## Impressions

Composed. Observant. The kind of owner who knows every table in her 
establishment and whose business it is. She intervenes when necessary 
and otherwise lets people get on with things. The Goat runs well 
because she runs it well.
