# Ryvar Thornbrae

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Ryvar directly*  
*Usually found: Loggers' & Timberfell Cooperative guildhall, Mill Road*

---

Human Guildmaster of the Loggers' & Timberfell Cooperative, regulating cutting rights
throughout the Northwood Stands and portions of the Sanctum Hills. Practical and well
respected, with responsibilities extending well beyond simply buying and selling timber.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. The party hasn't had reason to deal with the Cooperative or its
guildmaster.

## Reputation

Sets harvest quotas, oversees seasonal access, and mediates disputes between loggers,
landholders, and wardens — a steady hand on one of the trades Bridgeport's economy depends on
most heavily.
