# Gar Red-Phoenix

*Status: Not yet encountered*  
*Usually found: Phoenix Forge, Trellen Gap*

---

An ironbark ursinor affiliated with the Smiths', Armorers' & Metalworkers' Consortium, Gar
owns and operates Phoenix Forge — the source of most of the tools that keep Trellen Gap
functioning: axes, saws, nails, wagon fittings, palisade ironwork, and weapons or armor when
the need arises. His connection to the wider Consortium is maintained mostly through
occasional caravans rather than regular oversight, but he holds himself to guild standards of
craftsmanship regardless.

Calm and deliberate, Gar speaks little while working and prefers action to unnecessary words,
unimpressed by bluster and unmoved by loud claims. Once he commits to a task it gets done
properly. His size and evident strength lend the hamlet a quiet measure of security, and
though not a soldier by trade, he takes the safety of Trellen Gap seriously even without
saying so aloud.
