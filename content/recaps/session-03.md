# Session 03 — The Sinking Road, The Chalk Marks, and a Question Dorna Didn't Want to Answer
*Frostwatch, Day 3, Year 700AG*

---

## Guild-Certified, For Real This Time

A week passed while Bo-Bo waited on her retest. When it came, she
walked in and passed clean, no drama. Bo-Bo is Guild-certified. For
real this time.

---

## Dorna Bitterbuckle's Contract

With everyone finally registered, the party caught up with **Velra
Salspaena** at her usual spot in the Goat. She checked their
credentials, liked what she saw, and handed them off to **Dorna
Bitterbuckle**, who met them that same afternoon with a map, four
flagged locations, 50 gold up front, and 150 gold waiting on the other
end of a finished report. Straightforward contract work: structural
damage, recurring, somebody wants to know why.

---

## Copper Lane

First stop was Copper Lane, where **Rella Coppins** handed over her
unofficial notes on the road that keeps sinking no matter how many
times it gets filled back in. She also mentioned the grates — a way
down into the sewers nobody had bothered to use. The party dropped
down through one at a nearby intersection, quick and careful, nobody so
much as slipping. Clean descent, no incidents.

Underground, Bo-Bo's Midnight Spirit came out to play — a
humanoid-sized spectral companion doubling nicely as a torch-bearing
shoulder monkey when you commit to the bit, and the table committed to
the bit.

---

## The Second Lock

They found one of the strange sewer doors almost immediately, plus a
second, nastier lock behind it. Bo-Bo picked the first one without much
trouble. The second one fought her the whole way, and she lost that
fight, snapping her pick off clean in the lock, rattled by the thing in
a way she didn't love admitting. Notable consolation prize: a chalk
marking on the wall beside the door that nobody in the party can read
yet, but that somebody at the table is absolutely going to remember.

They backtracked, locked what they could, and kept working the sewers,
chasing the direction the Copper Lane sinkhole seemed to be migrating
toward.

---

## Ashwell's Wharf Warehouse

That trail put them underneath Ashwell's Wharf Warehouse — and this is
where **Vail** had one of those moments where everything just clicked.
She felt the vibration in the ground and traced it through the pipe
network to this specific location, then pushed further and got a read
on the direction it was radiating from. No idea what's actually down
there, just that something is, and it's not nothing.

---

## Dorna's Flicker

Evening rolled around, and the party surfaced to report back to Dorna.
When they showed her a sketch of the chalk marking, Vail caught
something Dorna almost certainly didn't mean to show — a flicker of
recognition, gone almost as fast as it appeared.

Vail pressed. Ender backed her up. The rest of the table leaned in.
Dorna eventually gave ground — not a name, but a shape: an organization
running what amounts to a smuggling operation, except the goods are
legal and the Council of Trades already knows it exists. Nobody's hurt,
commerce isn't disrupted, so the Council looks the other way. That's as
far as she'd go.

When the party pushed to use the locked doors themselves to get down
into the lower levels, Dorna shut that down flat. She'd get them
access — her way, through old maps she needed to dig up first — but
that wasn't happening tonight. Come back in the morning.

The party let it go. Headed back to the Goat to close out the night.

---

## Notes

- The party is under contract to Dorna Bitterbuckle: a map with four
flagged locations, 50 gold paid up front, 150 gold on delivery of a
finished report on the recurring structural damage beneath Bridgeport.
- The chalk marking found near the second locked door remains
unidentified — but Dorna recognized it and isn't saying what it means.
- Dorna has confirmed, in general terms, that an organization is
running an unofficial but Council-tolerated smuggling operation beneath
the city. No name given.
- Dorna has promised the party proper access to the locked sewer doors
via old maps, once she's located them. The party is to return in the
morning.
