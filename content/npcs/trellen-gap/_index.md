# Trellen Gap

The people who shape daily life in [Trellen Gap]({{< relref "/world/trellen-gap" >}}), a frontier
hamlet deep in the Great Forest, roughly four hundred miles east of Bridgeport. None of these
figures have come up in the campaign yet.

- [Elira Vanneth](elira-vanneth) — Reeve of Trellen Gap
- [Deldar Navalur](deldar-navalur) — Deacon, Shrine of the Luminous Birch
- [Duenna Whitwood](duenna-whitwood) — Overseer, Whitwood Timber Camp
- [Gar Red-Phoenix](gar-red-phoenix) — Blacksmith, Phoenix Forge
- [Harlan Redgrove](harlan-redgrove) — Proprietor, Redgrove Millworks
- [Marra Noakes](marra-noakes) — Proprietor, Great Forest Sundry
- [Dorian Voss](dorian-voss) — Proprietor, Greenhook Exchange
- [Tamsin Brindlebarrel](tamsin-brindlebarrel) — Innkeeper, Clumsy Apple Inn
