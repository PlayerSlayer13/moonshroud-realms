# Dorna Bitterbuckle

*First encountered: The Strangled Goat, Frostwatch Day 3, 700AG*  
*Usually found: Dockworkers' Guildhall*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Dockworkers' Guild and one of the ten members of the Council
of Trades. Impossible to miss and difficult to dislike — a veteran dockworker who clawed
her way from rope-hand to Guildmaster, she greets everyone like a long-lost cousin and
laughs loudly enough to quiet a room. Her hands are calloused, her beard immaculate, and
her sense of humor legendary on the waterfront.

## What the Party Knows

Velra Salspaena checked the party's credentials at the Goat and handed them off to Dorna,
who met them that same afternoon with a map, four flagged locations, 50 gold up front, and
150 gold waiting on a finished report — straightforward contract work investigating
recurring structural damage.

That evening, when the party showed her a sketch of a chalk marking found at the site,
Vail caught something Dorna almost certainly didn't mean to show — a flicker of
recognition, gone almost as fast as it appeared. Pressed, Dorna gave ground: not a name,
but a shape — an organization running what amounts to a smuggling operation, legal goods
only, which the Council of Trades already knows about and tolerates since nobody's hurt
and commerce isn't disrupted. That's as far as she'd go.

When the party pushed to use locked sewer doors themselves to reach the lower levels,
Dorna shut that down flat. She'd get them access on her own terms, through old maps she
needed to dig up — not that night. The next morning she made good on it, producing a
badly incomplete map showing a mausoleum in the Godsfall Necropolis as the closest access
point to the west end of the Freehold.

## Impressions

Warm, direct, and clearly more informed than she lets on. Behind the good humor is
ironbound honesty and a fierce protectiveness toward her workers — and, evidently, toward
whatever she knows about the organization behind the chalk marks. She gives ground when
pressed, but only exactly as much as she chooses to.
