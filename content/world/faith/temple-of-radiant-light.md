# Temple of Radiant Light

The largest and most respected temple in Bridgeport, standing along Queensway Avenue with 
tall windows and pale stone walls that give the whole structure the impression of being 
filled with light. Despite its size, the sanctums within are serene rather than 
ostentatious — this is a place built for reading and reflection as much as worship.

The temple is dedicated to [Aard]({{< relref "the-gods#aard" >}}), and its function in the city goes well beyond 
Sunday devotion. Scholars, judges, scribes, and magistrates come here as a matter of 
routine, not piety — the temple doubles as one of the finest libraries in the Freehold, 
and its archivists maintain some of the most complete legal and historical records 
anywhere in Bridgeport.

## The Archive

Citizens visit for arbitration, for testimony they need recorded honestly, or simply for 
guidance from priests known for calm reasoning over comforting platitudes. The archive 
itself is open to anyone with legitimate cause to consult it, though the priests who 
oversee it are protective of the material in their care and unhurried about handing over 
access to strangers.

## Standing in the City

The temple's influence reaches well past its own doors. Council chambers, courts, and 
guildhouses across Bridgeport lean on its records and its reputation for impartial 
clarity more than any single institution has a right to expect. For a city built on 
commerce and compromise, the Temple of Radiant Light is one of the few places where 
"what actually happened" still counts for something.
