# The Brass Axiom

Near the geometric center of old Bridgeport, a few blocks south of the Godsfall River, the
Brass Axiom is among the city's oldest surviving temples and among its most intellectually
austere. Built of pale stone trimmed with brass fittings darkened by age, its clean lines and
carefully balanced geometry favor order and precision over ornamentation. It's devoted to
[Absot]({{< relref "the-gods#absot" >}}), Eternal of cause and effect, cosmic order, and the
unbroken chain — those who come here rarely seek comfort alone, but understanding, discipline,
or the resolve to act deliberately in the present.

The temple grounds hold one of the sacred Echo Labyrinths found in temples to Absot throughout
Elderland — a winding contemplative path of pale and dark stone where devotees walk in silence,
meditating on choices, consequences, and possible futures. Inside, brass lamps illuminate
chambers lined with astronomical instruments, water clocks, and carefully maintained civic
records, and at the temple's center rises the Hall of Measures, where contracts are witnessed
and oaths sworn beneath a domed ceiling of celestial diagrams. Surveyors, magistrates,
navigators, and engineers are common visitors, and many of Bridgeport's oldest roads and
property boundaries were originally measured outward from the temple itself.
