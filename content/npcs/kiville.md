# Kiville

*First encountered: Saltstone Square, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*

---

A tall man in a long duster coat with good boots. Well-dressed in a way 
that isn't quite right — as if someone described fashionable clothing to 
him and he had it made accordingly. He carries a device on a chain that 
resembles a pocket watch, which he checks frequently and with evident 
meaning.

He was found crouching beside the fountain in Saltstone Square, 
examining the stonework with the focused attention of someone who finds 
old stone genuinely interesting. He spoke to two local girls with the 
careful courtesy of a person who has learned that locals know things 
worth knowing.

## What the Party Knows

He gave his name as **Kiville**. He is currently staying at the 
**Dragonline Lodge**.

He is an observer, by his own description. He asks questions that are 
oddly specific — about the age of stones, about how long markets have 
occupied particular squares, about whether the ground has always held 
where it holds now. His answers to questions asked of him are polite, 
considered, and almost entirely unhelpful.

His reactions to members of the party — a simiah, a katari, a faerie — 
were genuine enough to be unsettling. Not rude. Not performative. Simply 
the reactions of someone who had never encountered such people before and 
found them genuinely remarkable.

When asked about his pocket watch, he explained that it helps him keep 
track of *his* time. Not *the* time. **Bo Bo** caught that distinction. 
No satisfying explanation followed.

His body language, facial expressions, and general manner suggest he is 
not being deliberately deceptive so much as operating from a context 
that doesn't quite map onto Bridgeport, Algar, or — possibly — this 
plane of existence.

## What the Party Doesn't Know

Quite a lot, apparently.
