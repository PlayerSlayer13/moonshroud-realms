# Shrine of the Iron Oath

Bridgeport's second-most influential temple, behind only the Temple of Radiant Light, and 
unmistakable from a distance — an iron relief of a warhammer set before a scroll, encircled 
by an unbroken ring, mounted above the front steps and visible from the river docks to the 
south.

The shrine is dedicated to [Kelleth]({{< relref "the-gods#kelleth" >}}), and its heavy stone walls house Bridgeport's 
principal site for binding oaths, formal accords, and contract disputes. Priests here act 
as neutral stewards during guild negotiations, ship captains' agreements, mercenary 
contracts, and civic business — anywhere a spoken word needs to carry the weight of steel.

## Oaths and Arbitration

Nothing here is enforced by magic. The shrine's authority rests entirely on reputation and 
conscience — which, in Bridgeport, still turns out to be enough for most people. Guilds, 
ship captains, and mercenary companies alike treat a contract signed at the Iron Oath 
differently than one signed anywhere else.

## The Training Yard

Fighters, guards, and the occasional visiting barbarian are common sights here, offering 
alms before dangerous missions or seeking clarity when duty and personal conviction pull in 
different directions. The shrine's training yard sees regular use for blessings of arms, 
vow renewals, and the swearing-in of new militia officers — as much a working part of the 
city's civic life as a place of worship.
