# Harg Broken-Eagle

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Harg directly*  
*Usually found: Riverbend Farmers' Union hall, the eastern farmland*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Riverbend Farmers' Union. A mountain of fur and muscle shaped
by long seasons under open skies. His ursine features make him imposing; his voice
carries the steady patience of someone who has spent a lifetime settling disputes among
stubborn landholders.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Harg's reputation is common knowledge among anyone connected to
Bridgeport's farming communities, but the party has had no direct dealings with him.

## Reputation

Rose from ranch-hand to Union leader through sheer dependability — when storms hit or
harvests failed, Harg was always the first to arrive and the last to leave. He values
honest labor and the dignity of feeding a community, and is slow to anger and quick to
listen. He is also quietly concerned about environmental shifts — unseasonal frosts,
strange patterns in river flooding, new pests — and asks questions about these things
that nobody has satisfactory answers to yet.
