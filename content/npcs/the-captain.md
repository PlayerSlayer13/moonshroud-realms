# The Captain

*First observed: The Strangled Goat, Duskgrain Day 23, 700AG*  
*Usually found: Unknown — observed at the Strangled Goat*

---

A ship's captain, observed sitting in a corner table at the 
**Strangled Goat** with three companions — a woman, a man, and a 
male ribbet, all bearing the look of sailors.

## What the Party Knows

**Vail's** Liminal Awareness flagged this man as the most immediate 
danger in the room during the party's first evening at the Goat. No 
interaction occurred.

**Bo-Bo** recognized him. She said nothing. He said nothing. The 
moment passed without acknowledgment from either side.

His name is not known to the party.

## Impressions

The kind of man Bruna Emberfall will quietly manage rather than 
directly confront. The kind of man whose table the room adjusts 
around without quite knowing it is doing so.
