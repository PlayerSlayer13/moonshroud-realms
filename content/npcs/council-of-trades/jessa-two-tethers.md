# Jessa Two-Tethers

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Jessa directly*  
*Usually found: Teamsters' & Wagonwrights' Registry hall, the road out of Bridgeport*

---

Guildmaster-Delegate of the Teamsters' & Wagonwrights' Registry. Earned her nickname
after saving a runaway team of draft horses using only two spare tethers and a knack for
bold improvisation. The story has followed her ever since, which suits her fine — she has
never minded being underestimated right up until the moment she solves the problem
everyone else was wringing their hands over.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Jessa's reputation is common knowledge among anyone who travels
Bridgeport's roads, but the party has had no direct dealings with her.

## Reputation

Quick-witted, road-worn, and endlessly inventive, she knows every shortcut, safe camp,
and dangerous bend along the Freehold's roads. She campaigns tirelessly for road repairs,
improved waystations, and training programs for inexperienced handlers. One of the more
approachable members of the Council, with a sharp sense of humor that surfaces at
unexpected moments.
