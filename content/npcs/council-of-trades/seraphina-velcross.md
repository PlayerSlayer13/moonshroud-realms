# Seraphina Velcross

*Known by reputation only — the party hasn't met Seraphina directly*  
*Usually found: Merchants' Exchange, Council chambers*

---

High Convenor of the Council of Trades and Guildmaster-Delegate of the Merchants'
Exchange. Seraphina is the most visible figure in Bridgeport's civic life — poised,
measured, and reputedly unshakeable, presiding over Council sessions with a calm that
rivals have described as either admirable or unnerving depending on which side of a
ruling they were on.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing firsthand. Seraphina's reputation is common knowledge among anyone who follows
Bridgeport's civic affairs, but the party has had no direct dealings with her.

## Reputation

Speaks softly, listens carefully, and is known for finding compromises that leave
everyone slightly dissatisfied — which in Bridgeport politics passes for wisdom. Her
command of negotiation is widely respected; her trust is widely understood to be
hard-won. The Merchants' Exchange under her leadership has maintained transparent trade
and consistent market oversight. She is devoted to Bridgeport's stability above any
individual merchant's interest, and takes anything that threatens the flow of commerce
very seriously.
