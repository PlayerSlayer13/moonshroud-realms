# Tir Morendhil

The Tir Morendhil, known on Orb as Umbral elves, are natives of the twilight realm of
Umbral. They developed apart from the peoples of Orb, shaped by a world of shadow, silence,
and enduring darkness.

This page covers the veil lineage, the smaller of the Tir Morendhil's two branches.

---

## In the World

### Veil Elves

The veil lineage is associated with boundaries, thresholds, and the forgotten roads that
cross Umbral's vast reaches. Veil elves are the founders and defenders of Mirk Dorah, an
ancient fortress-city built into a mountain gorge and governed by the Triad, an oligarchy
of three Great Houses.

### Character and Culture

Life in Umbral affords no margin for softness, and veil elven culture reflects it. It is
disciplined, watchful, and built to endure rather than to flourish. Every veil elf of
standing belongs to one of the Great Houses. Those who owe allegiance to no House are the
Unhoused. Veil elves believe in the Great Repose, in which thousands of Reposed elves rest
in vast halls.

## Playing a Veil Elf

*The following is for players creating a veil elf character using the Daggerheart system.*

### Ancestry Features

**Umbral Sight**
You can see clearly in darkness, magical or otherwise, up to Close range.

**Unbroken Line**
Once per session, when an effect would force you to move against your will or flee a scene,
you may mark a Stress to ignore it and remain exactly where you are.
